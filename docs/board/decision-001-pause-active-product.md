# Board Decision 001: Pause Active Product Development

Date: 2026-06-27

## Decision: Pause active product development

Neuroljus is paused as an active product/app. The repository, website, and NL-VISION demo remain available, but no new product features should be built until the board reopens product development.

Neuroljus is repositioned as a research and technical portfolio project by Elizabeth Ospina on privacy-first AI support for caregiver observation and communication support in non-speaking autism.

## Reason

The current repository demonstrates a prototype and public website, but it does not contain enough customer, clinical, research, institutional, privacy, or business evidence to justify continuing active product development.

The project contains valuable technical and narrative assets, especially NL-VISION, the privacy-first direction, multilingual presentation, and caregiver-centered framing. However, the current evidence does not support product launch language, institutional readiness, or claims that the system can understand, translate, or interpret autistic signals.

## What evidence is missing

- Customer interviews with caregivers, families, clinicians, schools, or support organizations.
- Evidence of repeated caregiver usage.
- Evidence of willingness to pay or institutional procurement interest.
- Pilot results or deployment reports.
- Clinical or scientific validation of camera-derived metrics.
- Research protocol, consent model, or ethics review.
- Privacy and data-protection documentation suitable for sensitive caregiver or child-related contexts.
- Clear definition of the primary user, buyer, and intended setting.
- Business model, pricing, and go-to-market evidence.
- Accessibility validation with target users.

## What assets remain valuable

- The repository and website.
- NL-VISION as a prototype exploring browser-based, on-device observation signals.
- The OpenAI-powered caregiver chat as an experimental support interface.
- The privacy-first positioning.
- The multilingual public presentation in English, Swedish, and Spanish.
- The founder narrative and caregiver-centered mission.
- The contact page for collaborators, researchers, and institutions.
- The GitHub workflow protecting NL-VISION core files.
- Existing references to future white papers or research materials if they become available.

## What is no longer being built

- No active product launch.
- No new app features.
- No institutional beta rollout.
- No clinical workflow product.
- No research-partner platform.
- No claims that Neuroljus understands, translates, or interprets autistic signals.
- No claims of medical, diagnostic, or health-signal interpretation.
- No expansion of the beta gate or protected app area.
- No new product roadmap until reopening criteria are met.

## What would be required to reopen product development

Product development may be reopened only after the board receives enough evidence to justify renewed execution. Minimum requirements:

- A clearly defined primary user and use case.
- At least 10 documented target-user interviews.
- Evidence that caregivers or relevant users would use the prototype repeatedly.
- A written privacy, consent, and data-handling model.
- A clarified non-diagnostic claim boundary.
- A decision on whether the project is caregiver support, research tooling, assistive technology, or clinical-adjacent software.
- A validation plan approved before new feature work begins.
- A documented product scope limited to one initial workflow.
- Board approval to move from portfolio prototype back to active product development.
