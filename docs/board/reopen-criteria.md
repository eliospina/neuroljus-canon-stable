# Reopen Criteria

Neuroljus is paused as an active product/app. Reopening active product development requires explicit board approval under the criteria below.

## Minimum Criteria

All criteria must be met before active product development can reopen.

1. At least 5 caregiver interviews are completed and documented.

2. The primary user is clearly defined.

   The definition must state whether the first user is a family caregiver, LSS caregiver, school/support staff member, clinician, researcher, or another role.

3. The first use case is clearly defined.

   The use case must describe one repeated workflow, one user, one setting, and the expected non-diagnostic outcome.

4. Safety language is clear and approved.

   Public and in-product language must state that Neuroljus does not diagnose, does not replace caregivers, does not infer inner states with certainty, and does not provide medical advice.

5. Privacy policy is ready for pilot.

   Before any pilot or active product use, the project must have a privacy policy covering data minimization, consent, storage, deletion, AI processing, sensitive data, and user contact.

6. Validation plan is written.

   The plan must define the assumption being tested, target participants, method, duration, success criteria, risk controls, and what evidence would stop or redirect the work.

7. Overclaiming language is removed.

   Public-facing materials must not claim or imply validated AI understanding, translation of non-verbal communication, health-signal interpretation, clinical readiness, institutional readiness, or product launch readiness.

8. At least one serious collaborator, advisor, institution, or pilot contact is identified.

   This must be a real person or organization with relevant expertise, user access, domain credibility, or pilot relevance.

9. Decision checkpoint is scheduled.

   The checkpoint must have a date, evidence packet, and decision options: keep paused, reopen narrowly, continue research only, or stop the proposed product path.

10. Reopening decision owner is explicit.

   Reopening requires approval from Elizabeth Ospina as founder and a recorded Neuroljus board decision. If external advisors are involved, their input must be recorded, but the final reopening decision remains a founder/board decision.

11. Robotics or embodied-care scope has extra safeguards.

   If the reopened scope includes robots, sensors beyond the browser prototype, embodied interaction, institutional environments, or vulnerable-person observation beyond private caregiver testing, the project must first document consent/assent, human override, sensory-safety limits, data minimization, failure modes, and stop criteria.

## Reopening Standard

Neuroljus may reopen only as a narrow, evidence-seeking product effort. Reopening does not authorize broad feature development, institutional rollout, clinical claims, or validated AI interpretation claims.

Reopening into robotics or embodied care requires an even narrower safety review. A robot must never be framed as a caregiver replacement or as an authority about autistic inner states.

The first reopened product scope must directly serve the canonical decision rule:

Does this help gather trustworthy, ethical, useful observations that move Neuroljus closer to understanding communication patterns without overclaiming?

## Required Reopen Decision Record

Any reopening must create a new board decision document in `docs/board/` containing:

- decision
- date
- evidence reviewed
- criteria met
- criteria not met
- approved scope
- safety language
- validation plan
- next checkpoint
- decision owner
