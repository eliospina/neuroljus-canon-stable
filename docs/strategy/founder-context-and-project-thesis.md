# Founder Context and Project Thesis

This document is the canonical high-context source of truth for Neuroljus. It explains why the project exists, what it is trying to become, what it must never claim, and how future work should be judged.

## 1. Founder Context

Neuroljus was founded by Elizabeth Ospina.

Elizabeth has lived experience as a caregiver in Swedish LSS settings, a Master's background in Economics, and has been building Neuroljus with AI support over many months. The project is not an abstract software idea. It comes from close contact with care environments, communication barriers, and the daily reality of supporting autistic people whose needs may not be expressed through speech.

That founder context matters because Neuroljus began from a human and ethical problem before it became a technical prototype.

Elizabeth should be understood as the first caregiver-witness in the Neuroljus model. Her observations, questions, and ethical instincts are not generic user feedback; they are situated care knowledge. Future work should preserve that origin by treating caregiver input as contextual evidence that must be handled with humility, privacy, and uncertainty.

## 2. Original Mission

Neuroljus was not created as a generic caregiver journal.

It was created to explore whether AI can help understand patterns of non-speaking autistic communication in a privacy-first, non-diagnostic, dignity-preserving way.

The original mission is not to replace human care, automate interpretation, or claim access to another person's inner state. The mission is to ask whether careful observation, contextual notes, ethical design, and AI-assisted reflection can help caregivers notice patterns that would otherwise remain difficult to see.

## 3. Core Thesis

The central thesis is:

AI may be able to support better understanding of non-verbal autistic communication patterns when combined with caregiver observation, contextual notes, ethical design, and within-person longitudinal data.

This thesis is exploratory. It is not proven by the current prototype. It requires evidence, careful boundaries, and a refusal to overclaim.

## 4. Non-Negotiables

- No diagnosis.
- No replacing caregivers.
- No claiming certainty about inner states.
- No exploiting vulnerable people.
- Privacy-first.
- Human dignity first.
- Autistic person first.
- Caregiver as interpreter and witness, not passive user.

These principles are not brand language. They are operating constraints. Any feature, claim, partnership, research direction, or product decision that violates them is outside the Neuroljus thesis.

## 5. What Neuroljus Is Trying To Become

Neuroljus is not a simple app.

Neuroljus is not only a website.

Neuroljus is not a medical device at this stage.

Neuroljus is not a generic journal.

Neuroljus is an applied research and prototype project that may evolve into tools for:

- caregiver observation
- communication support
- pattern documentation
- AI-assisted reflection
- future research collaboration
- eventual institutional pilots if evidence supports it

The project should remain honest about its current maturity while preserving the depth of the original mission.

## 7. Current Strategic Problem

Neuroljus has a strong mission but lacks a clear first adopted product.

The strategic danger has two sides:

- Overclaiming AI interpretation before evidence exists.
- Reducing Neuroljus too much into a generic caregiver diary and losing the original thesis.

Both errors would damage the project.

Overclaiming would create ethical, scientific, and trust risk. It would imply certainty that the project does not yet have.

Over-reducing would make Neuroljus safer on paper but strategically weaker, because it would erase the reason the project exists: ethical AI-assisted understanding of non-speaking autistic communication patterns.

## 8. Correct Strategic Direction

Do not kill the mission.

Do not overclaim the product.

Neuroljus should separate the layers clearly:

### Mission

Build ethical AI-assisted understanding of non-speaking autistic communication.

### Research Program

Study whether structured caregiver observations plus optional visual signals can reveal useful within-person patterns.

### Product 1

A safe caregiver observation and communication-support tool that collects notes, context, optional NL-VISION signals, and produces neutral summaries.

### Near-Term Action

Before building more product, Neuroljus should test one narrow observation loop with real caregiver input: one user, one repeated situation, one template, and one non-diagnostic reflection workflow.

See `docs/strategy/near-term-action-vision.md` for the short-term action path.

### Future

Only after evidence, Neuroljus may expand toward research, institutional pilots, or stronger AI models.

### Long-Horizon Care Intelligence

Neuroljus also has a 50-100 year research horizon: AI and eventual care robotics that can support neurodivergent people while keeping human relationship, consent, privacy, and dignity central.

This does not mean Neuroljus is currently a robotics product or validated assistive technology. It means the project should make present-day decisions that could still be ethically defensible in a future where AI systems and robots are more present in care environments.

See `docs/strategy/long-horizon-care-intelligence.md` for the canonical robotics and long-horizon framing.

This layered direction preserves the founding thesis while keeping the current product claims bounded.

## 9. Decision Rule

Every future feature must answer:

Does this help gather trustworthy, ethical, useful observations that move Neuroljus closer to understanding communication patterns without overclaiming?

If the answer is no, the feature does not belong in Neuroljus.

If the answer is unclear, the feature should not be built until the relevant assumption is tested.

If the feature improves appearance, complexity, institutional signaling, or AI capability without improving trustworthy observation, it is not strategically central.

## 10. Working Interpretation For The Repository

The repository should understand Neuroljus as a layered project:

- The mission is deeper than the current app.
- The current prototype is not proof of the thesis.
- NL-VISION is a prototype asset, not a validated interpreter.
- Caregiver observation is central, but it is not the whole mission.
- AI is a support layer for reflection and pattern exploration, not an authority.
- Future institutional or research work depends on evidence, not aspiration.

This document should be read before major changes to product language, research framing, homepage positioning, beta flows, or NL-VISION claims.
