# Experience → product layers (do not 1:1 protocolize)

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Author context:** Elizabeth Ospina — many lived care experiences  
**Status:** working method for Neuroljus (international)

---

## The problem

Elizabeth has **many** experiences. Starting to tell them all at once is emotionally and cognitively heavy. Documenting them for the project is valuable — but **not every incident should become a care protocol**.

A protocol is a portable routine (light, sound, distance, pause, notify).  
An experience can be trauma, pattern, ethics, research, or one hard day. Those are different layers.

---

## Four layers (keep them separate)

| Layer | Tool | What it is | Becomes a protocol? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Care memory** | `/labs/care-memory` | Short lived stories for the project (what happened, what it taught) | Almost never |
| **2. Pattern note** | `/labs/pattern-notebook` | Gesture → what eased / worsened (within-person patterns) | Sometimes inspires a *scenario* later |
| **3. Serious incident** | `/labs/serious-incident` | Authorized witness for mistreatment / neglect / reporting | No — reporting packet, not a routine |
| **4. Care protocol** | Care Room / Protocol Workspace | Deterministic `care_command_protocol_v0` for humans / home / robot | Only when a **repeatable care sequence** is clear |

Rule of thumb:

> Document freely. Protocolize only when the same soft sequence would help the person again.

---

## How to start when there are “too many” stories

Do **not** aim to empty your memory in one chat.

1. Pick **one** story this week (5–10 minutes).  
2. Put it in **Care memory** (title + what happened + what it taught the project).  
3. If it was mistreatment → also a **Serious incident** export for your reporting path.  
4. If you saw a repeatable gesture→relief → a **Pattern note**.  
5. Only if a calm sequence repeats → then open Care Room and shape a protocol.

Chatting with an AI can help you *start* a note. The durable home is the local lab — so the story survives model/vendor change.

---

## International project, local law

Neuroljus is **international** (SV / EN / ES, families and care across borders).

Reporting duties differ by country. Product language should:

- Speak first of **local reporting duty / inspectorate / legal counsel**  
- Give **Sweden as one example** (Lex Sarah, IVO) — not as the only world  
- Let the witness write their **jurisdiction** in the note

Never pretend Neuroljus replaces any country’s inspectorate.

---

## What “good for the project” looks like

- Many care memories and patterns over months  
- A smaller set of serious-incident exports when harm must be reported  
- A **few** strong protocols that robots/homes can inherit  

Depth of dignity > volume of protocols.
