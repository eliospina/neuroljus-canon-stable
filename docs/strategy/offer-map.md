# Offer Map

This document defines what Neuroljus can offer now without reopening active product development or overclaiming.

## Positioning

Neuroljus offers a research pathway for turning caregiver observations into structured, respectful, non-diagnostic knowledge over time.

It does not currently offer a clinical product, diagnostic tool, production care app, institutional dashboard, or validated assistive technology.

## Core Offer

Better observations first. Future care intelligence later.

Neuroljus helps explore whether caregiver notes, context, uncertainty, and optional local prototype signals can support better reflection about within-person patterns in non-speaking autism.

## Offers Available Now

### 1. Caregiver Interviews

Audience:

- family caregivers
- LSS caregivers
- school/support staff
- professionals close to care contexts

What is offered:

- a careful discovery conversation
- no clinical data collection
- no diagnosis or advice
- learning about observation, handoffs, uncertainty, and language

Desired outcome:

- identify one safe first workflow worth testing

### 2. Observation Method v0

Audience:

- caregivers willing to test structured reflection
- collaborators reviewing research design

What is offered:

- a non-diagnostic observation template
- prompts for context, uncertainty, response tried, and follow-up
- a way to review repeated entries over time

Desired outcome:

- learn whether structured observation improves clarity without increasing burden

### 3. NL-VISION Lab

Audience:

- technical reviewers
- researchers
- collaborators curious about local visual metrics

What is offered:

- a browser-based prototype for optional face/hand observation signals
- local metrics and export
- caregiver-support chat with strict non-diagnostic boundaries

Desired outcome:

- explore whether optional local signals can support reflection without implying interpretation

### 4. Research Collaboration

Audience:

- researchers
- institutions
- ethics advisors
- accessibility collaborators

What is offered:

- collaboration conversations
- research framing
- validation planning
- privacy and consent discussion

Desired outcome:

- define whether Neuroljus should remain research-only, become a narrow caregiver observation prototype, or seek a formal collaborator.

## Not Offered Now

Neuroljus does not currently offer:

- diagnosis
- medical advice
- emotion, pain, need, or communication detection
- caregiver replacement
- clinical deployment
- institutional pilots
- robotics prototypes
- production monitoring
- automated interpretation

## Public CTA Guidance

Preferred calls to action:

- "Join a caregiver interview"
- "Try the observation method" (`/observation-method`)
- "Explore NL-VISION"
- "Discuss research collaboration"

Avoid:

- "Start care automation"
- "Detect what someone feels"
- "Use Neuroljus in your institution"
- "Launch beta"
- "Connect a robot"

## Decision Rule

An offer belongs on the public site only if it creates evidence, protects dignity, preserves uncertainty, and can be explained honestly today.
