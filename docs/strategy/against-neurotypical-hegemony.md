# Against neurotypical hegemony in care (founder stance)

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Author context:** Elizabeth Ospina — years of care work in Sweden  
**Status:** ethics compass for Neuroljus (not a survey paper, not a medical claim)

---

## The pattern named

In institutional and everyday care, a recurring pattern appears:

- Staff who are **neuro-normal / neurotypical** treat **their workflow, schedule, and social comfort** as primary.
- The **neurodivergent person** — especially non-speaking people — is ranked second.
- Humans are sorted by **capability** as defined by neurotypical norms.
- Mockery, impatience, and “efficiency” become cover for **egemony**: one cognitive style rules the room.

This is not “all caregivers are bad.” It is a **structural habit**: the worker’s neurotypical reality becomes the measure of the day; the person’s reality becomes interruption.

Elizabeth’s lived judgment after years in Sweden: that habit is common enough to design against.

---

## What Neuroljus must never encode

| Harmful default | Neuroljus refusal |
| --- | --- |
| Optimize protocols for staff convenience first | Protocols are authored for **the person’s continuity and relief** |
| Rank humans by speech, speed, compliance | No capability scores; no “good client / difficult client” labels |
| Treat rejection as bad behavior | `rejection_signal` is a **safety exception**, not defiance |
| Let “caregiver authority” mean staff ego | Authority means **responsibility for the person**, always overridable toward dignity |
| Hope that a black-box AGI will magically fix culture | Future systems inherit whatever we encode now — ableism or dignity |

---

## Caregiver authority ≠ caregiver primacy

Neuroljus keeps `caregiver_authority=true` because **someone must hold safety and override**.

That does **not** mean:

- the neurotypical worker’s preferences outrank the person  
- the person’s inner world is less real  
- mockery or ranking by capability is acceptable “professional culture”

It means:

> The human closest to the person may pause, escalate, and rewrite the protocol — **in service of the person**, not in service of institutional ego.

Good caregivers already live this. Neuroljus is built so their witness can become structure that survives shift change — so a selfish default cannot quietly rewrite the room without a trail.

---

## On AGI and the hope to break hegemony

Hope that stronger intelligence will break neurotypical hegemony is understandable. It is also **not automatic**.

AGI (or any powerful model) trained on institutional notes, HR metrics, and “compliance” language can **amplify** ranking-by-capability.

Neuroljus’s job is narrower and harder:

1. Encode **person-first protocols** that refuse mind-reading and refuse staff-first optimization.  
2. Preserve **uncertainty** and **rejection** as legitimate signals.  
3. Keep audit trails local and human-readable so power is visible.  
4. Offer future adapters (home, robot, wearable) a contract that preserves caregiver settings **and** the person’s safety exceptions — not a productivity dashboard for staff.

If future AGI is to help, it should learn from **lived care that centers the person** — not from the hegemony that ranked them last.

---

## Product checks (use in review)

Before shipping a feature, ask:

1. Does this make the **person’s** sensory/space needs easier to honor — or only staff throughput?  
2. Could a manager use this output to **rank** people by “difficulty”? If yes, redesign.  
3. Does copy imply the AI “understands” the neurodivergent person? If yes, cut.  
4. Does “caregiver control” read as **staff supremacy**? Soften toward **responsibility for the person**.

---

## Closing

Neurodivergent people are not a lower layer of humanity. They are not props in a workplace story about efficient shifts.

Neuroljus exists so care intelligence infrastructure does not quietly reproduce that ranking — and so future systems, AGI included, inherit **dignity first**.
