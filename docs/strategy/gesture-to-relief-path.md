# Gesture → relief: founder path (non-diagnostic)

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Author context:** Elizabeth Ospina — caregiver witness  
**Status:** research horizon + product compass (not a medical claim)

---

## What this is about

Many autistic people Elizabeth has supported do not speak their needs. Care happens by **watching gestures for hours**: where a hand goes, how the body protects itself, what sound or light worsens things, what pressure or quiet eases them.

Pain and discomfort can be **multiple and hard to locate**. That does not authorize Neuroljus to diagnose genetics or “systemic structure.” It authorizes us to build tools that help caregivers **witness, structure, and share patterns** — with uncertainty preserved.

Each person is unique. Still, across care relationships, **patterns of connection** can appear: similar gestures in similar contexts, similar reliefs. Neuroljus should help notice *within-person* and carefully compare *across-care* only with ethics and consent.

The person does not need to know if a device is expensive. They live in their own cognitive reality. Our job is dignity and relief — not prestige tech.

---

## Wireless belts / bands (horizon only)

Idea: soft, wireless bands that report **local numeric signals** (movement, pressure range, skin contact presence — TBD with ethics) into the same envelope as NL-VISION:

`observation → caregiver interpretation → protocol → adapter (human | home | robot | wearable)`

Rules if ever pursued:

1. Caregiver and, where possible, assent/consent of the person.  
2. Human override always.  
3. No emotion labels. No pain certainty from the device alone.  
4. Cost is a procurement problem for institutions — not a value judgment about the person.  
5. Evidence cycle first (observation notes) before hardware.
