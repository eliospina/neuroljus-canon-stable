# Pain and sensory load in autism — research bibliography (v0)

**Status:** living note for founder research (not a Neuroljus clinical claim)  
**Updated:** 2026-07-29  
**Purpose:** starting points while Elizabeth gathers peer-reviewed evidence that autistic people’s pain/sensory experience is often **under-seen**, and in many measures **more intense or differently modulated**, than the old “pain indifference” myth allowed.

---

## How to read this field (honest summary)

The scientific literature does **not** say “every autistic person always feels more pain than every neurotypical person” as a single universal law.

It **does** increasingly say:

1. The old clinical stereotype of **pain indifference** is wrong or dangerously incomplete.  
2. Many autistic people show **hypersensitivity** (e.g. pressure pain, heat ratings) and **less efficient inhibition** of tonic pain.  
3. There is also **heterogeneity**: hyper- *and* hypo-reactivity appear across modalities and individuals.  
4. Expression of pain may look atypical — so caregivers who watch gestures for hours are often closer to the truth than staff who wait for neurotypical pain scripts.

Founder stance: lived care already knew the body can carry more than institutions admit. Studies are for **grounding**, not for replacing witness.

## Strong starting papers

| Paper | Why it matters | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Failla et al. / field review: *The Current View on the Paradox of Pain in Autism Spectrum Disorders* (Frontiers in Psychiatry, 2022) | Names the “paradox”: reported indifference vs experimental equal/greater responsiveness; sensory dysfunction hypothesis | https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2022.910824 · [PMC free](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9352888/) |
| Zhang et al.: *Abnormalities in pain sensitivity among individuals with ASD: Evidence from meta-analysis* (Acta Psychologica Sinica, 2021) | Meta-analysis (N≈822): overall thresholds mixed; **lower pressure pain thresholds** (more sensitive); **greater physiological responses** to pain | https://doi.org/10.3724/sp.j.1041.2021.00613 · [EN summary](https://journal.psych.ac.cn/acps/EN/Y2021/V53/I6/613) |
| *Indifference or hypersensitivity? Solving the riddle of the pain profile in individuals with autism* (PAIN, 2023) | Autistic adults: **higher pain ratings** to heat; **pronociceptive** profile; challenges indifference belief; urges caregiver awareness | https://doi.org/10.1097/j.pain.0000000000002767 |
| Scoping review: *Prevalence, Expression, Assessment… Pain in Autistic Children* (A&A Practice) | Challenges assumption of low sensitivity; comorbidities raise pain risk; expression differs → delayed care | https://doi.org/10.1213/xaa.0000000000002178 |
