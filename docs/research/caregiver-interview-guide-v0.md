# Caregiver Interview Guide v0

Purpose: learn whether Neuroljus should become a caregiver observation tool, and what a safe first workflow should be.

This guide is for discovery only. It is not a clinical assessment, product pilot, therapy session, or diagnostic process.

## Who To Interview

Start with 5-10 people from one or two groups:

- family caregivers
- LSS caregivers
- school/support staff
- researchers or professionals who work closely with caregivers

Do not mix every possible user into one conclusion. Record the role and setting for each interview.

## Opening Script

Thank you for speaking with me. I am exploring Neuroljus, a privacy-first research prototype about caregiver observation and communication support in non-speaking autism.

I am not testing a medical tool or asking for clinical data. I want to understand how caregivers currently notice, record, and communicate patterns over time.

Please avoid sharing identifying details about a child or vulnerable person. General examples are enough.

## Core Questions

1. In your care context, what kinds of situations are hardest to understand when speech is limited or absent?

2. Do you currently write notes, track patterns, or communicate observations to someone else?

3. What makes a note useful later?

4. What usually gets forgotten, missed, or lost between caregivers?

5. Can you describe one repeated situation where better observation might help?

6. What kind of language feels respectful when describing behavior or possible communication?

7. What language would feel harmful, too certain, or dehumanizing?

8. Would a simple structured observation template help, or would it add burden?

9. Would an AI summary of caregiver notes be useful? What would make it risky?

10. What data would you never want collected?

11. What would need to be true before you trusted a tool like Neuroljus?

12. If Neuroljus helped only one small workflow at first, what should that workflow be?

## Optional NL-VISION Questions

Only ask these after the caregiver understands that NL-VISION is a prototype and not a validated interpreter.

1. Would optional local visual metrics ever help you reflect on context?

2. What would make camera-based observation unacceptable?

3. Would you prefer no camera, caregiver notes only, or optional camera signals?

4. What consent or assent would be necessary in your setting?

## What To Listen For

Signals that Neuroljus may be useful:

- repeated situations where caregivers lack structured memory
- handoff problems between caregivers
- vague notes that are hard to interpret later
- desire for respectful summaries
- need for uncertainty-preserving language

Signals to stop or redirect:

- caregivers see no value in structured notes
- workflow burden is too high
- privacy concern dominates
- AI feels intrusive or overconfident
- the use case drifts toward diagnosis or surveillance

## Evidence Summary Template

For each interview, record:

- date
- role
- setting
- repeated situation mentioned
- current observation method
- pain point
- language concerns
- privacy concerns
- possible first workflow
- direct quote or paraphrased insight
- confidence level: low / medium / high

Do not record identifying person-level data.
